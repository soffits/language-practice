# What exists for each piece (checked 2026-11-17)

Findings that decided this tool:

| Piece | Found | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Whole app | No mature open source tool does exam practice with speaking and writing feedback; LibreLingo (AGPL) was archived 2026-06; Anki is cards only. ai-language-tutor (MIT, 5 stars, 2026-08): agent as tutor, plain files, daily plan, cards from own errors, speaking debt. | Own CLI; borrow the design, not the code. |
| Scheduler | **py-fsrs**, PyPI name `fsrs`, 6.3.3 released 2026-11-13, MIT, one dependency (typing-extensions), Python 3.10+. API: `Scheduler(parameters, desired_retention, learning_steps, relearning_steps, maximum_interval, enable_fuzzing)`, `Card()`, `Rating.Again/Hard/Good/Easy`, `scheduler.review_card(card, rating, review_datetime)` returns `(card, ReviewLog)`, `get_card_retrievability(card)`, `Card.to_dict()/from_dict()`, UTC only. ts-fsrs and fsrs-rs exist too but are not Python. | Adopt py-fsrs, pinned in `uv.lock`. |
| Card format | Anki's cloze markup `{{c1::text}}` is the de facto interchange format; AnkiConnect needs the desktop app. | Store Anki cloze text plus correction and say-it cards; no Anki dependency. |
| Task formats (checked 2026-11-17 against official pages and PDFs) | **CELPIP-General**: celpip.ca test-format page, served as Test Format & Scoring (https://www.celpip.ca/take-celpip/test-format/; canonical https://www.celpip.ca/test-format-scoring/): Listening 46-55 min, Reading 43-56 min, Writing 53 min, Speaking 15 min, 6 listening parts, 4 reading parts, 2 writing tasks, 8 speaking tasks, names as in the data. CELPIP-General Overview Study Pack 2026, Prometric (https://www.celpip.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CELPIP-General-Overview-Study-Pack-2026.pdf): writing email 27 min, survey 26 min, 150-200 words each. Speaking Pro: Target 9+ Study Pack 2026, Prometric (https://www.celpip.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Speaking-Pro-Target-9-Study-Pack-2026.pdf), Speaking Test Overview table: prep/response seconds for all 8 tasks match the data. Speaking `minutes` (5 each) is a planning budget, not an official time (the official total is 15 min). **TEF Canada**: lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr TEF Canada examination page (https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/candidate/test-evaluation-francais/tef-canada/examination/): writing A 25 min, 80 words minimum; writing B 35 min, 200 words minimum; oral A 5 min, oral B 10 min (15 total); listening 40 questions 40 min; reading 40 questions 1 h. Oral B's planned `minutes` was 8, now 10. The 10-question sets are our split of those totals. **TCF Canada**: France Education international TCF Canada page (https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada): listening 39 questions 35 min; reading 39 questions 60 min; writing 3 tasks in 60 min with 60-120, 120-150 and 120-180 words (no per-task minutes are published, so the 20/20/20 split is ours and no longer labelled official); oral 12 min including 2 min prep: task 1 2 min, task 2 2 min prep + 3.5 min, task 3 4.5 min. The examhero.ca page the TCF source pointed to is not official and was replaced. | Data files with these sources; re-check the numbers on the same pages when an exam changes. |
| Speaking analysis | [speech-check](https://github.com/soffits/speech-check), written alongside this tool. | Subprocess adapter. |
| Points | An external points ledger command (`earn --source`, idempotent, `undo --source`). | Command adapter; no trophy code here. |
| Voice files | A chat front end hands the assistant a path to the recording. | The assistant passes that path with `--audio`; nothing in this tool depends on a chat app. |
